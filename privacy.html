Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Aqar Factory ("we", "us") is a real-estate marketing and advisory business based in Cairo, Egypt. This policy explains what personal data we collect through aqar-factory.com, why we collect it and the choices you have.

What we collect

We collect what you choose to give us, plus basic technical data when you browse the site.

  • Enquiry and price-quote forms: your name, email address, phone number (if you add it) and the message you write.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • AI assistant: the messages you type into the chat, so that it can answer you.
  • WhatsApp and phone buttons: if you choose to message or call us, the conversation takes place in WhatsApp or on your phone, under their own terms.
  • Browsing data: pages visited, device and browser type and approximate location, collected through Google Analytics.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and arrange viewings.
  • To send the newsletter you asked for.
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve it.
  • To keep the site secure and to prevent spam and abuse.

We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run the site, and only as far as needed: website hosting and delivery (Vercel), our database server, image storage (Cloudflare), analytics (Google) and the AI service that powers the assistant. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Cookies and browser storage

Google Analytics sets cookies to measure traffic. Your browser also stores your language choice and a saved copy of our logo and name so pages load faster. We do not use cookies to show you advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site keeps working, but your visit will not be counted in our analytics.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and subscriber details for as long as we need them to deal with your request, and for a reasonable period afterwards, then we delete or anonymise them. Analytics data is kept according to the Google Analytics retention settings.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on servers we control. Access to our admin tools is limited to our team. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop contacting you, and you can ask to be removed from the newsletter at any time. Egyptian law, including the Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020), gives you rights over your personal data. To use them, contact us with the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
